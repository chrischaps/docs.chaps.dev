Polyend Tracker

Polyend · synth, 8 voices · channel 1

Polyend's 8-track sample tracker; over MIDI it plays one instrument as a synth and takes CC for its sound, mixer and perform effects.

From the maker's chart: Polyend Tracker Manual v1.7.0, ch. 11 MIDI (11.2 Configuration, 11.4 CC mapping, 11.9 MIDI Synthesizer), ch. 8 Master mixer, https://polyend-website.fra1.digitaloceanspaces.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/01084159/Polyend-Tracker-Manual-1.7.0.pdf

Before you play

Set Config > MIDI: Notes In and CC In to USB or MIDI In jack, and Notes Input Channel / CC In channel to match this profile (each is All or Ch 1-16; the manual gives no factory channel, so 1 is an assumption). Stop the sequencer to play it as a MIDI synthesizer: notes play the last selected instrument. Synth CCs 1-31 and 83-118 act on that instrument; CC 51-62 drive Perform mode effect slots, 71-82 the mixer. The Tracker receives no program change.
