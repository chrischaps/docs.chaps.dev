Module water_body 

Source
Expand description

Water on any mesh (ADR 0094): lakes at their own level, rivers that run and fall, each with its own tint, drawn with the ocean’s shader.

A body is a mesh of WaterVertexes, each carrying its rest position, its surface normal and a flow (XZ, m/s). The ocean’s vertex shader has a second entry point, vs_body, that displaces such a mesh with the ocean’s wave spectrum (scaled by the body’s wave_scale) and advects the waves and foam along the flow. The fragment shader is the ocean’s own, so Fresnel, refraction, the cel ramp and the shoreline foam (ADR 0088/0091) all carry over. Fast water foams in streaks along its flow.

Bodies come from two places: the water_body scene component (a lake’s outline or a river’s path), and crate::SceneRenderer::set_water_body for a game’s own mesh. Both draw after the sea, depth-tested, so a lake above sea level hides the sea under it.

Functions§

mesh_from_component
Build the mesh a water_body component describes: shape = "lake" with an outline of [x, z] points and a level, or shape = "river" with a path of [x, y, z] points, a width, a speed and a fall_speedup.
river_points
A river’s points from a centre line with one width and speed, the speed rising at the foot of a drop: speed × (1 + fall_speedup × slope), the slope being the drop over the run of the segment upstream of the point. So water is calm at a fall’s lip, fastest at its foot, and slows again over the next segment.
