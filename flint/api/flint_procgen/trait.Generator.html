Expand description

A procedural content generator.

Implementations produce GeneratorOutput (mesh, image, or sound) from a ProcGenSpec and a deterministic SeededRng. Generators are stateless — randomness lives in the RNG, parameters in the spec.

Object-safe: Box<dyn Generator> works.
