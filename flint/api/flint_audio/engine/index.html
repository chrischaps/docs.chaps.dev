Audio engine wrapping Kira’s AudioManager

Handles sound loading, listener management, and spatial track creation. Degrades gracefully when no audio device is available.

The mix runs in one of two places (#136). On the desktop, and in the browser’s fallback, kira runs here on its own backend ([Mixer::Local]). In the browser it normally runs in an AudioWorklet, off the main thread: every call below then becomes a command to that mixer, and each sound crosses to it once, as samples ([Mixer::Remote], crate::remote). Handles (SoundHandle, SpatialTrack) hide which.

Constants§

DECODE_NOW_BYTES
A queued sound asked for before its turn is decoded on the spot when its file is this small (a one-shot: about 12 ms in the browser at most), and skipped while it moves to the front of the queue when it’s bigger.
DEFAULT_DISTANCES
Distances for a placed one-shot when the caller names none: full volume within 1 m, silent past 25 m.
DEFAULT_ROLLOFF
How a placed sound fades between its distances when the caller names no curve. With r the listener’s fraction of the way from min to max, the sound is -60·r^rolloff dB: at 2 it holds up most of the way and drops near max; below 1 it falls fast past min and tails off slowly, near an inverse-distance law (0.6 between 32 and 1250 m is within 1.5 dB of (32/d)^1.2 out to 300 m).
MAX_ONESHOTS_PER_SOUND
How many placed one-shots of one sound may play at once. One more steals the oldest, which fades out over STEAL_FADE: raft creaks and drips come in bursts, and by then the oldest has mostly died away (#138).
STEAL_FADE
The fade a stolen one-shot stops with.
