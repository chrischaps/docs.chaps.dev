Module pan 

Source
Expand description

Placed sounds as plain panned voices (#138).

A kira spatial track works out a sound’s distance falloff and each ear’s share for every sample it mixes: on the Pixel 8 Pro’s main thread that was most of a 15 ms mix, and natively a spatial track costs about 14 times a plain voice (examples/mix_bench.rs). Here the same gains are worked out once a frame, from the same formulas, and handed to an ordinary sound as a volume and a constant-power pan, which kira applies per sample for almost nothing. The listener and the sources move a few centimetres a frame, and each change is tweened over the frame, so the ear can’t tell. A stereo file keeps its stereo instead of being folded to mono, the one difference; Float’s are all mono.

Falloff
A placed sound’s falloff: full volume within min metres of the listener, silent past max, -60·r^rolloff dB between, with r the listener’s share of the way from min to max (see DEFAULT_ROLLOFF).
